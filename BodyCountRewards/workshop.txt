title=Body Count Rewards
description=[i]Finally, a mod where your body count actually matters.[/i]
description=Look, I'm not here to judge your... [i]numbers[/i]. Kill zombies, hit milestones, earn trait rewards. Higher trait cost = lower drop chance. Simple.
description=
description=[b]Compatible with B42.20+ (Stable) | SP and MP[/b] because your body count shouldn't depend on your social life.
description=
description=The spiritual successor to Circuit's [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3540255691]Kill Milestones (Zombies) B42[/url]. His mod gave me the idea, I wanted more features and control for my server, so I built my own from scratch. Circuit has since moved on from PZ modding and linked this mod on his page. Thanks for the inspiration!
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Stats UI:[/b] Three tabs for progress tracking, full reward history, and a trait catalog with rarity and conflict info
description=[*] [b]Right-click context menu:[/b] Quick-check kills and distance to next milestone. Toggleable in Mod Options.
description=[*] [b]Smart trait conflicts:[/b] Mutually exclusive traits are resolved automatically. The Catalog tab shows exactly why any trait is unavailable.
description=[*] [b]43 traits[/b] in the pool (21 earnable, 22 removable). Every single one individually toggleable in sandbox settings.
description=[*] [b]Third-party addon API:[/b] Other mods can register their own traits via [b]BCR.RegisterCustomTraits()[/b]. See the addons below for working examples.
description=[*] [b]Toggleable context menu:[/b] Mod Options in the ESC menu lets you disable the right click context menu.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox Options[/h2]
description=All configurable in-game by the host. No file editing needed.
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Kill threshold[/b] per milestone (2 to 10,000)
description=[*] [b]Progressive or linear[/b] scaling
description=[*] [b]Reward priority:[/b] Remove negatives first, add positives first, or coin flip
description=[*] [b]Grant Missed Opportunities:[/b] Install mid-game with thousands of kills? Get all owed rewards at once
description=[*] [b]Positive rewards, negative removal, or both.[/b] Your body count, your rules
description=[*] [b]Individual trait toggles:[/b] Enable or disable any of the 43 traits
description=[/list]
description=
description=Drop chances scale with trait cost: cheap traits (1-2 pts) drop often, expensive ones (7-8 pts) almost never. Check the Catalog tab for more info.
description=

description=[h2]FAQ[/h2]
description=
description=[b]Q: Do I keep rewards if I remove the mod?[/b]
description=A: Yes. Traits are permanently saved to your character.
description=
description=[b]Q: Thousands of kills but only 1 reward?[/b]
description=A: Enable "Grant Missed Opportunities" in sandbox options and kill one more Z to trigger the update.
description=
description=[b]Q: Why can't I get trait XY?[/b]
description=A: Right click > BodyCountRewards > Catalog tab. It tells you exactly why.
description=
description=[b]Q: Linear vs Progressive?[/b]
description=A: Linear = same kills per milestone. Progressive = each one needs more kills. Scaling factor controls how steep it gets.
description=
description=[b]Q: Athletic/Strong/Fit?[/b]
description=A: Maybe. Those grant perk points, so they need extra work. If enough people want it, I'll look into it.
description=
description=[b]Q: Works with mod traits?[/b]
description=A: Yesn't. Not out of the box, but modders can use the addon API. Check out the addons linked above.
